## Description

PlayerPath is the easiest way for grassroots, semi-professional, and non-league football players to connect with clubs actively recruiting talent.

Think LinkedIn × Transfermarkt — for football.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOR PLAYERS:
• Build a complete football CV — positions, statistics, club history, achievements
• Upload highlight videos to showcase your talent
• Get discovered by clubs searching in your area
• AI-powered match scoring shows your compatibility with clubs
• Apply to open trials with one tap
• Share your profile as a professional PDF

FOR CLUBS:
• Search and filter players by position, age, height, stats, county, and step
• Discover local talent with geo-radius search
• Post open trials and manage applications
• Build shortlists and track prospects
• AI matching engine finds players who fit your system

• Verified clubs and player profiles
